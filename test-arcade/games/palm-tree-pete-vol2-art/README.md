# Rescue from Palm Tree Pete's Island 2: The Great Escape

Volume 2 of [Rescue from Palm Tree Pete's Island](https://ploug76.github.io/escape-from-palm-tree-petes-island/) (Volume 1 is unchanged). A FIVE8NINE Studios game.

**Play:** https://ploug76.github.io/escape-from-palm-tree-petes-island-vol-2/

- **Quest:** 5 worlds (Moonlit Beach, Jungle Trail, The White Villa, The Striped House, The Getaway Docks), 30 stages, with a VS boss fight at the end of every world (Big Sal, Jungle Jake, Butler Boris, Gatekeeper Gus and Palm Tree Pete himself). 3 stars per stage, saved progress.
- **Stealth:** hide in bushes, stay out of flashlights, searchlights and security cameras, crouch behind crates, and sneak up behind goons to bonk them. Running near a goon is noisy. Free every kid from the cages and lead them all to the exit.
- Story intro and ending, with a big red SKIP on every page.
- Jointed characters (knees and elbows), with steps tied to how far they move.
- **Touch, no on-screen D-pad:** drag left or right anywhere to walk (drag farther to run), swipe up to jump, tap to bonk or open a cage, drag down in a bush to hide, drag up or down on a ladder to climb. Keyboard (arrows, Shift, Space, X) and game controllers (USB/Bluetooth: stick, A jump, X action, B hide, Start pause) fully supported, menus included.
- Works offline (PWA) and updates itself on open.
