# Helene's Candy Quest

A match-3 beside [Helene's Treasure Chest](https://ploug76.github.io/helenes-treasure-chest/) and [Volume 2](https://ploug76.github.io/helenes-treasure-chest-vol-2/). Those games are not changed.

The pieces are the same jewels Helene holds in her chest. Swipe or tap to swap neighbors. Lines of 3 or more clear, candies fall, and cascades count. Match 4 makes a Ribbon, an L or T makes a Wrapper, and match 5 makes a Chest Star.

18 levels, with a goal and a move limit on each. Stars and best scores stay on this device (`hcq_save_v2`). A finished quest can post to the shared leaderboard (`helenes-candy-quest__quest`).

Open `index.html`. On a phone, swipe the board. Arrows and Space work on a keyboard.
