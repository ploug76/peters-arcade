# Helene's Candy Quest

A match-3 beside Helene's Treasure Chest and Volume 2. Those games are not changed.

The pieces are old-fashioned hard candies from Helene's chest: a red-and-white peppermint swirl, a green striped pillow, a yellow raspberry drop, a purple ribbon, an orange cut-rock flower, and a blue sour ball.

Swipe or tap to swap neighbors. Lines of 3 or more clear. Match 4 makes a Striped Stick, an L or T makes a Wrapped Candy, and match 5 makes a Candy Dish.

18 levels, each with a goal and a move limit. Stars and best scores stay on this device (`hcq_save_v2`). A finished quest can post to the shared leaderboard (`helenes-candy-quest__quest`).

Open `index.html`. On a phone, swipe the board. Arrows and Space work on a keyboard.
