# Pete's 420 Adventure

Review copy. Adults only, 21+. Not linked from the arcade hub, and not for merge until Peter says so.

Rebuilt on the Jimothy Timothy shell: swipe anywhere to move, green ACTION on the left, orange JUMP on the right, the same title, menus, and HUD, and the painted backdrops from Jimothy Timothy and the Hair Mob. The Counter Pal is that raccoon, sunglasses included. Pete is drawn in that same style from Peter's features: short graying hair, blue eyes, and a gray goatee, in a flannel shirt. The photo itself is not in the game. The budtender, the grower, and the host are separate sprites.

The 21+ gate is the first screen. Play does not start until the player taps "I AM 21 OR OLDER." The badge stays on the title, the HUD, and the ending.

Three rooms, same errands as the earlier review:

1. Dispensary District. Turn the pipes until water reaches the plant. The Counter Pal is the wrong window. The budtender gives a Seed Pouch.
2. Grow House. Turn the tiles until light reaches the plant. The moth is just visiting. The grower gives a Lounge Token. It is not money.
3. Chill Lounge. Swap the kit until the joints glow: tip, paper, flower, twist. The parrot is the wrong bird. The host gives a Chill Stamp. The couch is the ending.

Nothing has a price. Nobody but Pete has a name.
