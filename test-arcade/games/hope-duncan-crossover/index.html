Hope & Constable DuncanIsolated crossover prototype. The main game is unchanged.

Hope wears a fitted black long-sleeve shirt, black baseball cap, black aviator sunglasses, and blue jeans. Her long brown ponytail catches criminals, swings the ravine, and strikes with forgiveness.

Save the world from evil. Switch between Hope and Constable Duncan. Duncan keeps the on-foot actions verified in the original game: walk, run, jump, and ACTION to bust.

  • Move: ← → or A D. Hold Shift, or hold a direction, to run. Swipe and hold anywhere. A long swipe runs.
  • Jump: ↑, W, or K, or swipe up, or JUMP. A run-jump does not cross the ravine.
  • Switch: C or SWITCH.
  • Hope punch: Space or J, or PUNCH. One tap punches. Two taps punch twice. Three taps inside 0.45s coil the ponytail, then unroll it. Contact is a blast of forgiveness, and it does not also fire the punches.
  • Hope prayer: Q or PRAY. A prayer blast knocks evil aside.
  • Hope grace: E or GRACE. Grace Abounding knocks evil aside in a wide wave.
  • Hope lasso: F or LASSO. The brown ponytail catches a criminal, or swings her across the ravine from the near edge.
  • Duncan action: Space, J, or ACTION. Busts a crook within 90 and folds a closed umbrella within 84. Running into a fleeing crook within 38 nabs him.
